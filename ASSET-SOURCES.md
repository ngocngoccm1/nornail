## Fonts

DM Sans is self-hosted from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License). Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans . The license file is saved in `assets/fonts/`. See the update below for the heading font.

## Design research

- Ardoury: https://www.ardoury.de/ — inspected desktop rendering, HTML and CSS: large split hero, serif and sans contrast, a restrained palette, product-like editorial sections. No source imagery or code reused.
- Townhouse: https://townhouse.co.uk/ — inspected desktop rendering and page structure: focused navigation and visible calls to action. No source imagery or code reused.
- TEN&TINT: https://tenandtint.com/ — inspected desktop rendering, HTML and CSS: minimal content hierarchy and short explanatory copy. No source imagery or code reused.
- Supply reference: https://angelnails.no/ — inspected product and navigation structure. No imagery reused.

## Photographic direction update (Townhouse reference)

Desktop and mobile versions of Townhouse were inspected again on 2026-10-21, including the photographic hero, services carousel, product shelf, product detail story and footer. The Nornails redesign adopts a bright neutral ground, large real photography, restrained serif headings and horizontally browsable collections. No Townhouse photography, logo, testimonials, product claims or code are used.

New editorial images are free stock photographs from Pexels, used as illustrations of nail work; they do not depict a verified Nornails salon, team, service result or owned product. The actual product catalogue remains based on the supplied products and Maiwell originals.
